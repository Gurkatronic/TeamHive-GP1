# HORROR BAR // Last Orders — Free Sample

Blind Meridian | Version 1.0.0

A usable selection from Horror Bar: 15 unique models, two ready-closed ingredient bottles and one assembled drink.
These are the same textured exports as the full pack. There are 18 GLBs, 18 FBXs and eight required PNG textures.

## Start here

1. Extract the ZIP. Keep `Exports` and `Textures` together so FBX relative image paths resolve.
2. Import the models from `Exports/GLB` or `Exports/FBX`. GLBs have embedded textures.
3. Repeat the straight counter, use end caps at the ends, place stools, and build a small wall/floor section.
4. Add Black Brine and Tonic Water from `Variants/Ingredients`. `Assemblies/HB_Drink_LowTide` is a ready-garnished drink.

`Docs/Sample_Asset_Catalog.csv` lists dimensions, pivots and sockets. `Docs/Import_Notes.md` explains material setup.
The preview frames each item separately for readability; it does not indicate relative physical sizes.

## Included

- Straight counter, end cap, stool, 2m wall and 2m floor.
- Tumbler glass; apothecary bottle and cork; mixer bottle and crown cap; Veinfruit wheel garnish.
- Low, medium and high tumbler liquid inserts, plus an ice cube.
- Closed Black Brine and Tonic Water bottles, and the Low Tide assembled drink.
- Required runtime textures and the sample inventory.

The free sample has no Blender source or furnished scene. The full pack includes 78 unique models, the complete
ingredient selection, booths, tables, lighting tiers, additional surfaces, posters, editable Blender sources,
and the furnished showcase. The full pack already includes every asset in this sample.
